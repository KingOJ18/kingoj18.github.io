Who We Are

Named for a legacy of service

BENASOD Foundation takes its name from Rev. Dr. Benson Asane Odogu — a life devoted to compassion, generosity, and service to others. As his children, we founded this foundation to preserve and extend that legacy, turning a family value into an organization that reaches far beyond our own household.

Every program we run and every life we touch is guided by the same conviction that shaped our father’s life: that dignity, opportunity, and hope belong to everyone, regardless of circumstance.

Our Story

From one man’s compassion to a movement of hope

Rev. Dr. Benson Asane Odogu spent his life quietly meeting needs — a school fee paid here, a family fed there, a young person counselled through a difficult season. He never sought recognition; he simply believed that hope, once offered, has a way of multiplying.

After his passing, his children came together with one question: how do we make sure this doesn’t end with him? BENASOD Foundation is our answer — a formal, sustainable structure built to carry his compassion into communities we haven’t reached yet, and into generations still to come.

Today, BENASOD Foundation supports the needy, invests in education, runs training and mind-coaching programs, and partners with communities on initiatives they help shape themselves. What began as one man’s quiet generosity is now a foundation built to put smiles on people’s faces, one life at a time.

We measure our progress not in the size of our organization, but in the number of people who can say their circumstances changed because someone showed up for them.

Our Vision

Bringing hope to the needy and the hopeless while creating opportunities for individuals and communities to thrive.

Our Mission

To put smiles on people’s faces by empowering lives through compassion, education, support, training, and sustainable community development.
